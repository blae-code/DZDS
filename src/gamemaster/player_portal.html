Badlands Survival Field Manual · 5 Pillars

Nasdara is a brutal desert sandbox where dehydration, infected wounds, and raider ambushes claim careless survivors in minutes. Master these five operational pillars to endure.

1. Water Purification & Cholera

SUSTENANCE

Untreated badlands surface water, irrigation canals, and stagnant ponds carry lethal cholera bacteria. Drinking unpurified water induces vomiting, rapid dehydration, and death within minutes.

Boiling Water: Boil water in a Cooking Pot, Kettle, or Mess Tin over a campfire or gas stove for 100% sterile water.
Chlorine Tablets: 1x Purification Tablet in a Canteen or Water Bottle instantly sterilizes contaminated water.
Cistern Perk: Fountains inside Qalat-e Lut fortress [148 078] carry 0% cholera risk thanks to the aquifer project!

2. Homestead Farming & Curing

AGRICULTURE

Canned rations are rare in the wastes. Cultivate persistent garden plots and cure wild pelts using active server farming rules:

Non-Rotting Crops: BetterHarvest is active with allowPlantSpoilage = 0. Cultivated crops never rot on the vine! Fast growth: Zucchini (22m), Tomatoes (27m).
CookZ Non-Decaying Food: Smoked sausages, cheese wheels, and canned rations never decay (EnableCheeseDecay = 0).
Bedouin Tanning: Combine wild boar/deer pelt + Garden Lime in a water barrel to produce Tanned Leather for Saddles (8x) and Bridles (4x).

3. Trauma & Infection Triage

MEDICAL

Desert wounds turn septic quickly under the scorching sun. Follow Bohemia's 3-stage infection triage protocol:

Stage 1 (Dirty Wound): Disinfect immediately with Alcoholic Tincture, Iodine, or Disinfectant Spray within 15 minutes.
Stage 2 (Wound Sepsis): Blurry vision, fever, and steady health drain. Requires a full course of Tetracycline Antibiotics.
Stage 3 (Septic Shock): Emergency IV SalineBagIV + Epinephrine + repeated Tetracycline required to arrest collapse.
Fractures: Craft a Wooden Splint (4x Wooden Sticks + 1x Bandage or Rag) to mend broken legs in 15 minutes.

4. Settler Radio Net 87.8 MHz

INTELLIGENCE

The desert provides zero visual warnings before raider war parties close in. Keep tactical communication active:

Tune to 87.8 MHz: Your personal radio is pre-tuned to Settler Net 87.8 MHz. Install a fresh 9V battery and switch it ON.
Automated Bulletins: Radio broadcasts alert survivors to active dust storms, AI convoy movements, and trade outpost status.
Short, Dark Nights: Nights on Ad Aspera are pitch-black but short (~15–20 minutes real time). Keep flares, headtorches, or campfires ready.

5. 5-Tier Respawn Network

LOGISTICS

After death, Ad Aspera routes your next survivor through a smart 5-tier respawn hierarchy:

1. Qalat-e Lut Fortress HQ: Primary safe respawn inside the fortress citadel across 13 candidate staging spots (daily column rotates at 11:00 UTC / 06:00 US Central).
2. Friend-First Co-op: When allies are online, respawn places you within 1–2 km of a living player to eliminate lonely desert walks.
3. Raised Base Flag: Wakes directly beside your compound's raised Territory Flag (up to 40-day active refresh).
4. Pitched Wilderness Tent: Wakes beside any pitched tent within 5 km of death.
5. Roadside Campfire: Wakes near your previous death point beside a warming campfire.

Light Vehicles & 24-Hour Cooldown Fallback

Full-size vehicles carry a 24-hour real-time cooldown per player to prevent suicide gear farming. If you die within the cooldown window, you will never be stranded on foot:

Respawn automatically provides a Light Vehicle: an Enduro Dirtbike 250N (Motorbike_01), Scout Motorcycle TT650 (Motorbike_02), or a saddled Badlands Steppe Horse with Western Saddle and Bridle!

Light vehicle spawns do not trigger or extend the 24-hour cooldown timer.

Chop-Shops & Zen's Static Car Workbench

Craft ZenStaticCarWorkbench with 60× Nails + 5× Metal Sheets at your compound. This industrial workbench repairs ruined vehicle components:

  • Completely restores ruined engine blocks, radiators, and body panels to pristine condition.
  • Re-trues and vulcanizes damaged vehicle wheels and rims without replacement spares.
  • Radiator Critical Warning: Radiators must be 100% filled with clean water; running dry in desert heat destroys the engine block in under 90 seconds!

Expansion Keys, Lockpicking & Camo Tarps

All vehicles enforce physical security through DayZ-Expansion-Vehicles:

  • Physical Keys: Every spawned vehicle pairs with a physical key in your pocket (VehicleRequireKeyToStart = 1). Master key pairing is supported.
  • Lockpicking: Locked doors can be picked with Lockpicks (10–25% success rate per attempt). Lock cylinders can be replaced.
  • CarCover Tarps: Deployable camouflage tarps conceal vehicles from aerial reconnaissance and distant snipers.
  • Car-to-Car Towing: Towing cables allow any working rig to tow immobilized vehicles back to base for repair.

Territory Flag Protection & @DZDS Respawn

A raised TerritoryFlag creates a 60 m protection sphere (Immersive Placing). Placed item lifetimes increase from 24 hours to 7 days, and the flag refreshes nearby structures for 40 days.

On death, DZDSBases.c checks for living allies within 1,000 meters. If none are near, your survivor wakes directly beside your compound's nearest raised flag.

PvE Security, Digital Locks & Integrity

All doors and gates accept native Vanilla 1.30 and RaG DigitalCodeLock (battery-free, auto-locks on close, remembers your code). Infected (PvZmoD) can bash open unlocked doors but cannot breach locked gates.

AI faction patrols do not dismantle player bases. Building the RaG Hunting Cabin consumes/ruins 1 Hammer on completion.

Install & run the app (PWA & off-LAN links)

Install Ad Aspera as a standalone desktop or mobile Progressive Web App with offline caching and persistent per-player settings.

AInstall on desktop (Linux, Windows, macOS)

  • Chrome / Edge / Brave / Vivaldi: open the Command Center App & Share tab and click Install App, or click the install icon (⊕) in the browser address bar. You can also plan compounds in the Interactive Basebuilding Planner.
  • Firefox Desktop: the Service Worker caches the map and guide offline automatically; use PWAsForFirefox or your system WebApp Manager for a dedicated window.

BInstall on mobile (iOS, iPadOS & Android)

  • iOS & iPadOS (Safari): tap Share (↑) → Add to Home Screen → Add.
  • Android (Chrome / Firefox / Samsung): tap Install App in the banner or open the browser menu (⋮) → Install app / Add to Home screen.

CRun the app from the terminal

From the repository root on the host PC, start the local server or launch an off-LAN tunnel:

make dashboard OPEN=1
make dashboard SHARE=1 OPEN=1

Or run .venv/bin/python tools/dashboard.py --share --open directly.

DOff-LAN share links & personal preferences

Players outside the host's LAN can connect using an invite link (/?share=...) created in the App & Share studio or open a portable single-file snapshot.

Persistent per-player settings: each visitor gets an isolated profile with their own callsign, theme, map layers, pinned strongholds, custom waypoints, and site field notes saved across sessions.
