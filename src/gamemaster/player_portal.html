Getting in

Prepare the map and use a launcher that can read the complete mod list.

1Own Badlands

Nasdara is part of the Badlands DLC. Players need to own the DLC to join; the server does not.

2Use a compatible launcher

  • Windows: find Ad Aspera in DZSA Launcher and let it install the required mods.
  • Linux: add the server to DZGUI using its query address below.
23.229.49.100:26092

The number above is the query port for finding the server in DZGUI, not the game port.

3Join the server

Search for Ad Aspera in DZSA or DZGUI, install its full mod list, then connect. The server is password-protected; get the password from the host.

Why not the official launcher? Its server browser can truncate this server's mod list. Use DZSA on Windows or DZGUI on Linux instead.

4Pack for the first miles

New arrivals begin at a shared refugee column site. Bring a physical map and compass: your map does not show your live position.

First days in the sand

Small habits matter more than a marker on a screen.

Find your people

First arrivals on the same in-game day wake at the same column site. It rotates daily at 11:00 UTC (06:00 US Central Standard Time).

After a death, your next survivor is placed near another living player when possible; otherwise, respawn favors a roadside point near your previous death.

Tune the Settler net

Your arrival radio is tuned to 87.8 MHz and starts switched off. Fit its battery, switch it on, and listen for world bulletins.

A vehicle is available at most once per 24 hours; a death before that cooldown expires may mean no vehicle on the next life.

Read the landscape

Most of Nasdara is still open country. Choose when to enter a fight.

Strongholds and war zones

Faction patrols and conflict events gather around strongholds and active war zones. Away from them, the land is mostly vanilla survival; a rare war party may cross open country.

Travel the hard way

  • Use the physical map and compass to plan your route.
  • There is no player-position marker or map fast travel.
  • Town names and faction control can change as the war ledger advances.
