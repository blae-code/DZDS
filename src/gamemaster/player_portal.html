Getting in

Prepare the map and use a launcher that can read the complete mod list.

1Own Badlands

Nasdara is part of the Badlands DLC. Players need to own the DLC to join; the server does not.

2Use a compatible launcher

  • Windows: find Ad Aspera in DZSA Launcher and let it install the required mods.
  • Linux: add the server to DZGUI using its query address below.
23.229.49.100:26092

The number above is the query port for finding the server in DZGUI, not the game port.

3Join the server

Search for Ad Aspera in DZSA or DZGUI, install its full mod list, then connect. The server is password-protected; get the password from the host.

Why not the official launcher? Its server browser can truncate this server's mod list. Use DZSA on Windows or DZGUI on Linux instead.

4Pack for the first miles

New arrivals begin at a shared refugee column site. Bring a physical map and compass: your map does not show your live position.

First days in the sand

Small habits matter more than a marker on a screen.

Find your people

First arrivals on the same in-game day wake at the same column site. It rotates daily at 11:00 UTC (06:00 US Central Standard Time).

After a death, your next survivor is placed near another living player when possible; otherwise, respawn favors a roadside point near your previous death.

Tune the Settler net

Your arrival radio is tuned to 87.8 MHz and starts switched off. Fit its battery, switch it on, and listen for world bulletins.

A vehicle is available at most once per 24 hours; a death before that cooldown expires may mean no vehicle on the next life.

Read the landscape

Most of Nasdara is still open country. Choose when to enter a fight.

Strongholds and war zones

Faction patrols and conflict events gather around strongholds and active war zones. Away from them, the land is mostly vanilla survival; a rare war party may cross open country.

Travel the hard way

  • Use the physical map and compass to plan your route.
  • There is no player-position marker or map fast travel.
  • Town names and faction control can change as the war ledger advances.

Basebuilding & Homesteading Field Manual

Every structural recipe, storage system, farming cycle, and vehicle workshop from Vanilla DayZ 1.30 and all active mods on Ad Aspera.

Territory Flag Protection & @DZDS Respawn

A raised TerritoryFlag creates a 60 m protection sphere (Immersive Placing). Placed item lifetimes increase from 24 hours to 7 days, and the flag refreshes nearby structures for 40 days.

On death, DZDSBases.c checks for living allies within 1,000 meters. If none are near, your survivor wakes directly beside your compound's nearest raised flag.

PvE Security, Digital Locks & Integrity

All doors and gates accept native Vanilla 1.30 and RaG DigitalCodeLock (battery-free, auto-locks on close, remembers your code). Infected (PvZmoD) can bash open unlocked doors but cannot breach locked gates.

AI faction patrols do not dismantle player bases. Building the RaG Hunting Cabin consumes/ruins 1 Hammer on completion.

Badlands Homestead Progression Arc

Stage 1: Camp & Cache

First 48 Hours: Pitch an Improvised Stick/Tarp/Leather Shelter (100 slots), build a 16-stone Camp Oven, and bury a Wooden Crate or Leather Sack stash.

14 days stash · 45 days shelter

Stage 2: Home on Wheels

Nomad Rig: Claim an Apocalypse Truck, Semi Truck, or Loaf Van. When parked, cloak the vehicle with a CamoNet via CarCover.

Persistent while covered

Stage 3: Canyon Outpost

Modular Shelter: Craft the RaG Book or combine 4 Planks + 10 Nails into Foundation, Wall, Door, and Roof kits in protected cliff pockets.

14 days base · 40 days w/ Flag

Stage 4: The Cabin

Homestead Milestone: Construct the lockable log Hunting Cabin with 8 shuttered windows, indoor stone fireplace, and wall-switched lights.

45 days · 100% refund dismantle

Stage 5: Compound & Garage

Workshops & Storage: Outfit with MMG Gun Walls, 300-slot Supply Crates, Zens Car Workbench, Horse Stables, and non-rotting Garden Plots.

10–28 days base · Refreshed by Flag

Stage 6: Settlement Network

Flag & Respawn: Raise a Territory Flag (60m Immersive Placing radius) to unlock @DZDS Base Respawn and claim a Frontier Settler garrison.

40-day Flag · Off-screen protected

Install & run the app (PWA & off-LAN links)

Install Ad Aspera as a standalone desktop or mobile Progressive Web App with offline caching and persistent per-player settings.

AInstall on desktop (Linux, Windows, macOS)

  • Chrome / Edge / Brave / Vivaldi: open the Command Center App & Share tab and click Install App, or click the install icon (⊕) in the browser address bar. You can also plan compounds in the Interactive Basebuilding Planner.
  • Firefox Desktop: the Service Worker caches the map and guide offline automatically; use PWAsForFirefox or your system WebApp Manager for a dedicated window.

BInstall on mobile (iOS, iPadOS & Android)

  • iOS & iPadOS (Safari): tap Share (↑) → Add to Home Screen → Add.
  • Android (Chrome / Firefox / Samsung): tap Install App in the banner or open the browser menu (⋮) → Install app / Add to Home screen.

CRun the app from the terminal

From the repository root on the host PC, start the local server or launch an off-LAN tunnel:

make dashboard OPEN=1
make dashboard SHARE=1 OPEN=1

Or run .venv/bin/python tools/dashboard.py --share --open directly.

DOff-LAN share links & personal preferences

Players outside the host's LAN can connect using an invite link (/?share=...) created in the App & Share studio or open a portable single-file snapshot.

Persistent per-player settings: each visitor gets an isolated profile with their own callsign, theme, map layers, pinned strongholds, custom waypoints, and site field notes saved across sessions.
