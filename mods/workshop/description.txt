[h1]Ad Aspera Factions (DZDS)[/h1]
The custom faction mod for the Ad Aspera friends-only PvE server. You only need it to play there; the server's mod list fetches it for you.

[h2]What it adds[/h2]
[list]
[*]Five factions for DayZ Expansion AI: Frontier Settlers (players), UN Peacekeepers, the Jackal Cohort, the Karkas Mountain Clan and the Rust Syndicate
[*]Runtime diplomacy: truces and betrayals between factions, driven by the server's war ledger
[*]Reputation tolerance: the Karkas and the Rust spare individual players they have come to trust
[*]A server-side world layer for the server's Game Master
[/list]

[h2]Requires[/h2]
[list]
[*]Community Framework
[*]DayZ-Expansion-Core
[*]DayZ-Expansion-AI
[/list]
