[h1]Ad Aspera Factions (DZDS)[/h1]
The custom faction and living-world mod for the Ad Aspera friends-only PvE server on Nasdara (Badlands 1.30). You only need it to play there; the server's mod list fetches it for you.

[h2]What it adds[/h2]
[list]
[*]Five factions for DayZ Expansion AI: Frontier Settlers (players), UN Peacekeepers (Task Force Blue Shield), the Jackal Cohort, the Karkas Mountain Clan, and the Rust Syndicate
[*]Runtime diplomacy: truces, lockdowns, and betrayals between factions, driven by the server's persistent War Ledger
[*]Reputation tolerance: the Karkas and the Rust spare individual players whose Hardline faction reputation has earned their trust
[*]Diegetic Settler Net radio bulletins (87.8 MHz): war reports, front-line shifts, and trader updates delivered exclusively to players carrying a powered, switched-on radio
[*]Camp & territory-flag respawn registries (DZDSCamps, DZDSBases) and server-side world markers showing which faction holds each stronghold or contested warzone
[/list]

[/list]
